01 / Featured project

Pull It Together

Get the wagon home.
Keep the team together.
Stay ahead of the storm.

One to four players haul a wagon across a procedurally generated wilderness, gather supplies, and respond to dynamic encounters. Traversal, resource management, and an approaching superstorm keep cooperation at the center of every run.

I lead the technical design and implementation of the pacing director, world generation, multiplayer interactions, animation synchronization, and supporting tools.

03 / Building for iteration

Make the system
easier to inspect.

Tools that connect an authored change to a visible result.

Terrain & level preview

I built a preview path for inspecting generated layouts before running a full session. Leaving environmental scatter out of the preview keeps the focus on terrain and level structure.

Post-game analysis

Run summaries bring together a map, event history, and target-versus-proxy pacing plots. These views help me inspect what happened and connect system behavior back to design intent.

Resolved generation profiles

Editable presets resolve into stable session inputs. Separate seed domains keep unrelated random choices from unnecessarily affecting one another.

Research direction / Early exploration

Intent-guided playable world authoring

I’m exploring how natural-language requests and direct controls could guide the creation of playable worlds. The question is how to keep design intent editable while preserving authored regions and gameplay constraints through regeneration.

This proposed workflow builds on my existing generation and pacing systems. LLM integration and constraint-preserving editing are planned extensions.

06 / About & contact

Building systems.
Working with people.

I hold an M.S. in Game Design and Development from Rochester Institute of Technology and am pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence. My work combines technical implementation, hands-on game design, and research-oriented prototyping.

Before game development, I served in the U.S. Air Force as an instructor, evaluator, analyst, and program manager. That experience shapes how I explain complex systems, coordinate work, and support a team.

On Pull It Together, I work with our core developers and external artists and composers, alongside responsibilities in our studio’s business operations.
